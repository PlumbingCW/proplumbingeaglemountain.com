Failure signs

Signs the tank itself is done, not just a fitting

A fitting leak is a repair. These signs point inside the tank, where nothing can be patched.

Seam or jacket weeping

Water that tracks down the outside of the steel after the drain valve and top fittings are dry. The lining has pitted through.

Rust-colored hot water only

Cold taps run clear while hot ones run brown. The tank interior is corroding and flaking.

Wet insulation under the jacket

Soaked foam when a technician lifts the cover means the shell has been leaking for a while.

Repeat repairs on a worn tank

A second element, a second thermostat, and a ticking tank add up to a heater near the end of its service life.

Why a tank gives out from the inside first

A steel tank is lined with glass, and the lining slowly cracks as the tank heats and cools. Water reaches the bare steel through those cracks, and the anode rod can only protect so much of it. Once pitting passes through the wall, the leak starts as a damp spot and grows into a steady drip.

That is why a leak that began small can turn into a flood on a busy washing day. If water appears but the tank itself is dry, the source may be a hidden pipe, so ask about pinpointing a hidden leak.

Side by side

Fix the part or buy a new tank

Repair makes sense when

  • The leak comes from a fitting, valve, or flex connector.
  • The tank is quiet, clear of rust, and still heating well.
  • The failed part is one standard component, like an element or a thermocouple.
  • You plan to move or renovate soon and want a short bridge.

Replace when

  • Water leaks from the shell or the base seam.
  • Rust shows in the hot water or around the drain valve.
  • The tank is old enough that the next failure is likely soon.
  • You want a different size, fuel, or type before the next breakdown.

The process

What happens the day your old tank comes out

1

Shut down and drain

We close the supply, kill power or gas, and drain the failed tank to the floor drain or outdoors.

2

Remove and prepare

The old tank is disconnected and carried out. Corroded nipples, flex lines, and shutoff valves are replaced as needed.

3

Set and connect

The new tank goes on a level base or in a drain pan, with vent, relief valve, and discharge pipe set to the manufacturer's instructions.

4

Fill, test, and explain

We fill the tank, bleed air at a hot tap, light or power the unit, confirm temperature, and show you the controls.

Worth knowing

Pick the capacity by first-hour rating, not just gallons

A tank's first-hour rating tells you how much hot water it delivers in the first 60 minutes from a full, hot start. A 50 gallon gas tank often beats a 50 gallon electric one on that number because the burner recovers faster. For a busy family, the rating tells you more than the label capacity.

Tell us how many people shower in the morning and whether a soaker tub is in the house. We match the replacement to that load.

Questions about water heater replacement in Eagle Mountain

Do you handle water heater replacement emergencies at night or on weekends in Eagle Mountain?

Yes. Dispatch at (385) 488-1892 is staffed after hours, and same-day visits are possible when openings exist. Close the cold valve, cut power or gas, and give us the tank size.

How long does a typical tank swap take?

Many like for like swaps fit into a single visit, depending on venting and old connections. Moving a heater, adding a vent, or switching fuel takes longer. We tell you the plan before we start.

My water heater is leaking from the bottom. Can it still be repaired?

If the water is coming from a drain valve or top fitting, yes, and repair is the better call. Water from the shell or the base seam means the tank has failed.

Can I switch from a gas tank to a heat pump unit during an emergency swap?

It is possible, but it needs an electrical check, floor space, and a condensate route, so most people restore hot water first and plan the switch for later.

Do you remove and haul away the old water heater?

Yes, if you ask. We drain it, disconnect it, and carry it out so you are not left with a wet, heavy tank in the garage.

What size replacement tank do I need?

Match the first-hour rating to your household's busiest hour, not only the gallons. Tell dispatch how many people use hot water in the morning and we size from there.

Will my new heater have a warranty?

New tanks come with a manufacturer's warranty that varies by model. Ask for the terms for the unit you choose, and keep the data plate photo and your receipt.
