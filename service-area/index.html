Silverlake and other newer streets, with a caution

New streets and subdivisions are added faster than maps update. Silverlake is one such name, and if your map app does not show your street correctly, give the dispatcher a nearby landmark such as Porters Crossing or Cedar Valley High School. We confirm each address before dispatch.

If your home is under construction, our plumbing installation page covers rough-in and fixture work. Ask the Eagle Mountain Building Department at 801-789-6614 which permits your project needs.

Telling dispatch exactly where you are

A large share of Eagle Mountain is newer subdivisions and larger lots, and the same street name can appear in more than one phase. Give dispatch the full street address, the nearest cross street and the neighborhood name, such as The Ranches or City Center, so the plumber heads to the right place first time.

If your home sits at the end of a long driveway or behind a gate, say so on the call. Mention a gate code, a dog in the yard or where the main water valve is. Those details save minutes when water is running.

Calls from Saratoga Springs work the same way. Tell dispatch your ZIP code, 84045 for most of the city, and the nearest main road, and the plumber can plan the drive along Pony Express Parkway.

Saratoga Springs and the drive along Pony Express Parkway

Pony Express Parkway runs east from Eagle Mountain to Redwood Road in Saratoga Springs, with links to Pioneer Crossing and I-15. The distance is roughly 10 miles, about 20 minutes by road, as an estimate. Our Saratoga Springs page covers ZIP 84045, local water and sewer, and what to tell the dispatcher.

Lehi sits north of Saratoga Springs. Cedar Fort and Fairfield are also in our wider region. Callers there can ask the dispatcher whether a visit is possible, since coverage is confirmed street by street.

Winter, freezes and why location matters

Eagle Mountain is at roughly 4,900 feet, and Utah winters bring hard freezes. Homes on exposed streets lose heat faster in garages and attics. If a pipe splits, our burst pipe repair page explains the steps, and the first move is always to close the main valve.

Drinking water for the city comes from five groundwater wells and surface water from the Central Utah Water Conservancy District. Ask the Water Department for its annual drinking water quality report if you want local details.
