Tankless water heater work in Eagle Mountain, UT

A tankless water heater in Eagle Mountain, UT heats water only while a tap is open, so it has no storage tank to run dry or to leak. Gas units fire a burner through a heat exchanger. Electric units use heating elements, and both measure the flow before they start.

We install new units, service existing ones, and flush scale out of the heat exchanger. Tank-style heaters fall under water heater repair instead. Same-day service visits are possible when openings exist.

Cold water in the middle of a hot shower, and the name for it

People describe tankless trouble in plain terms, such as hot water that fades when a second tap opens, bursts of cold between hot, or a display that flashes a number. Those symptoms usually trace to flow, sizing, scale, or ignition.

A fast fix starts by matching the symptom to the technical term. Low flow at the tap points to a clogged inlet screen or scale. Cold-hot-cold swings point to a flow sensor, an undersized unit, or a recirculating loop that cools the supply.

The process

Flushing and descaling a tankless heater, step by step

1

Shut off and isolate

Turn off gas or power and close the isolation valves on the hot and cold lines.

2

Connect the pump and hoses

Hoses run from the service ports on the isolation valves to a bucket and a small pump.

3

Circulate a descaling solution

White vinegar or a descaler made for heat exchangers runs through the unit for the time the manufacturer sets.

4

Rinse and restore

Flush with clean water, clean the inlet screen, reopen the valves, and test the unit for output.

Why scale builds up, and why flushing is not optional

Minerals in any water supply cling to the hot surfaces of a heat exchanger. Even a thin layer cuts heat transfer, so the unit runs hotter, cycles on high limit, and throws errors. In Eagle Mountain the city publishes no hardness figure in its annual drinking water quality report, so test your tap or ask the Water Department for the report.

Follow your manufacturer's flush schedule. Units without isolation valves with service ports cannot be flushed correctly, and we can add them during a service visit.

Side by side

Tankless or a tank heater, honestly weighed

Where tankless wins

  • Hot water keeps coming as long as the unit can keep up.
  • Wall-mounted units free floor space in the garage or closet.
  • No stored water to leak and flood a room.
  • Some models can be serviced by flushing instead of replacing.

Where a tank wins

  • Lower upfront gas, venting, and wiring changes.
  • Simple repair with common parts.
  • Steady hot water during a brief power loss on a gas tank.
  • Several taps at once without flow limits.
Worth knowing

Flow rate and temperature rise decide whether one unit is enough

Every tankless heater has a maximum flow rate at a given temperature rise. The rise is the gap between incoming cold water and your target. Utah winters bring cold incoming water, so the same unit that keeps up in July may fall short in January.

Add up the gallons per minute of the taps that run together, such as a shower plus a dishwasher. If the total beats the unit's rating at your winter rise, you need a larger unit or two.

Questions about tankless water heater in Eagle Mountain

Why does my tankless water heater give cold bursts in the shower?

Cold-hot-cold swings are usually a flow issue. The unit may be sized too small for the taps in use, an inlet screen may be blocked, or the flow sensor may be failing. The technical name is minimum activation flow, and we test it.

How often should a tankless heater be flushed and descaled?

Follow the manufacturer's schedule, which varies by model. Scale depends on your water, so test your tap or ask the Water Department for its annual drinking water quality report.

What does an error code on a tankless heater mean?

Each brand uses its own numbers, but they fall into families such as ignition, flow, overheating, and vent faults. Read the code and model to dispatch and we can narrow it down before we arrive.

Does a tankless water heater need a bigger gas line?

Often. The burner uses more gas at full fire than a tank burner does. We size the line to the unit's BTU rating and check what your meter can supply.

Can I convert my tank heater to tankless?

Yes, but plan for gas, venting, and wiring changes. Our team can walk through each change before you commit.

My tankless heater is not heating at all. What should I check first?

Confirm the gas valve and the power are on, and check for a display code. If the unit still fails, consider water heater repair for a diagnostic.

Can a tankless water heater freeze in a Utah winter?

Yes, if it is on an exterior wall or in an unheated space without protection. Many units include freeze protection, but it depends on power staying on and on correct installation.
