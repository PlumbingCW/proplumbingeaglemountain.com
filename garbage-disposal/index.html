Symptoms

What the noise or silence under your sink means

Disposals send clear signals when they fail. Match what you hear to the cause.

Humming but nothing turns

The grinder is jammed by a bone, fruit pit, or utensil. Power it down and release it with the hex socket on the bottom.

Click or total silence

The reset button on the base may have tripped, or the circuit or outlet has lost power.

Water under the unit

A leak at the top flange, the dishwasher inlet, the drain outlet, or the unit body points to different repairs.

Slow drain with the disposal on

The clog is past the disposal in the trap or branch line, and no amount of grinding will clear it.

Loud grinding or rattling

A foreign object, worn shredder ring, or loose mounting can make the noise, and it is rarely fixed by running water.

Smell that comes back

Food residue in the splash guard or a partly blocked drain holds odor, and cleaning only works if the line is open.

When a flooding sink or leaking disposal needs help tonight

A disposal leak soaks the cabinet floor quickly, and a backed-up kitchen sink can overflow onto flooring. If the leak is steady or the sink is overflowing, call the dispatch line. After-hours and same-day visits are offered when available.

  1. Switch off the disposal at the switch and at the breaker before touching anything.
  2. Never put a hand into the grinder, even when it is off, and use tongs for a stuck item.
  3. Turn off the angle stops under the sink if the leak is on the supply side.
  4. Bail standing water from the sink and put a bucket under the leak.
  5. Do not pour drain cleaner into a jammed disposal, since the chemicals splash on whoever works on it.

How we replace a disposal and what to expect

Replacement starts by disconnecting the power, the dishwasher hose, and the drain trap, then releasing the old unit from its mounting ring. If the mounting is still sound, the new unit clips into it. Otherwise a new sink flange goes in with fresh plumber's putty or a gasket.

A new disposal needs a cord or hardwired connection, which is why we check the outlet and the switch. A unit connected to a dishwasher also needs its knockout plug removed, or the dishwasher will back up. We test it with water running before we leave.

What to avoid

  • Grease and oil, which cling inside the trap
  • Pasta, rice, and potato peels, which swell into paste
  • Bones, pits, and shells that jam the grinder

The process

How to clear a jammed disposal safely

1

Cut the power

Switch it off at the wall and unplug it or flip the breaker so it cannot start while you work.

2

Turn the grinder by hand

Insert a hex key into the socket on the bottom and work it back and forth until it moves freely.

3

Remove the jam

Use tongs or pliers from above to pull out the object. Never use your hand.

4

Press reset and test

Push the red button on the base, restore power, and run cold water. If it hums again, call a plumber.

Questions about garbage disposal repair and replacement in Eagle Mountain

Do you handle garbage disposal emergencies at night or on weekends in Eagle Mountain?

Yes. Leaking, jammed, and overflowing disposals are taken by dispatched plumbers on nights and weekends through our 24/7 dispatch line. Cut the power first. Same-day appointments run when our schedule has room.

Why is my garbage disposal humming but not grinding?

The grinder is jammed, or the motor is failing. With the power off, turn the hex socket on the bottom by hand. If it frees and still hums, the motor may be worn.

How long does a disposal usually last?

Many last around a decade with careful use, though it varies with the model and what goes in. A unit that leaks from the body or hums after clearing is near the end of its life.

Can you install a disposal where there was none?

Often yes. It needs a nearby outlet or a new circuit and a compatible sink drain. An electrician may be needed for the power, and a plumber handles the sink side.

Why does my sink back up when the disposal runs?

The trap or branch line past the unit is blocked. The disposal only chops waste, so drain cleaning on the kitchen line fixes it.

Is it safe to use chemical drain cleaner with a disposal?

We advise against it. The chemicals can splash during repair, and they do little for a grease blockage. A snake or a jet clears the line without that risk.
