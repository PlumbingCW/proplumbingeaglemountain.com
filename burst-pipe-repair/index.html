A burst pipe in Eagle Mountain, UT needs the water off first

A burst pipe in Eagle Mountain, UT is an emergency because water can soak drywall, flooring and insulation within minutes. We dispatch 24/7, and while you wait you should close the main shutoff valve and open a faucet at the lowest point to drain the line.

Burst pipe repair means cutting out the split, cracked or blown section and replacing it with sound pipe and proper fittings. The goal is a permanent fix, not a clamp that holds until Tuesday.

Water pouring from a broken pipe right now

Every minute counts, so do the shutoff before anything else. Dispatch is 24/7 including holidays, and same-day appointments are offered when available for damage that is contained and can wait a few hours.

  1. Turn the main shutoff valve clockwise until it stops. If it is stuck, do not force it with a tool that could break the valve.
  2. Open the lowest faucet in the house, such as a basement or garage sink, to let the line drain.
  3. Turn off the water heater power or gas so the tank does not heat while empty.
  4. If water is near outlets or a panel, stay out of the wet area and leave the breaker alone.
  5. Move belongings away from the flow and take photos for your insurance claim, then call (385) 488-1892.

Cracked or broken pipe inside a wall, ceiling or floor

When the break is out of sight, the first job is locating it. Wet drywall, a bulging ceiling or a sudden loss of pressure all point to the run that failed. A small inspection cut lets the plumber see the pipe and size the repair.

We open only what the repair needs and keep the opening square so a drywall patch is simple. If the break reveals more pinholes along the same run, we tell you before you pay for a third patch. In that case repiping part of the house may be the better call.

Copper pipe repair: splits, pinholes and bad joints

Copper pipe is durable but it has limits. A freeze splits it along its length, corrosion eats pinholes into the wall, and a weak solder joint can let go under pressure. Each failure leaves a different pattern, which tells us whether it is a one-time event or a trend.

The repair cuts back to clean, sound copper, then joins new pipe using soldered fittings or press fittings, depending on the space and the wet conditions. The work gets pressure tested before any wall is closed up, so a drip does not return behind new paint.

Drain pipe repair when the break carries wastewater

A cracked drain line is a different problem from a burst supply line. It leaks only when a fixture drains, so the damp spot comes and goes. You might smell sewer gas or see a stain under a bathroom, a laundry or a kitchen.

Most drain pipe in newer homes is plastic, glued at the joints. A loose joint or a crack from settling is replaced section by section, and the new run needs correct slope and venting. For a failure out in the yard, see sewer line repair.

Frozen pipes in Utah winters: thaw safely and stop the burst

Utah winters bring hard freezes, and Eagle Mountain sits at roughly 4,900 feet, so pipes in garages, exterior walls and unheated rooms are at risk. A frozen pipe often shows as a faucet that gives only a trickle or nothing at all. Do not wait for it to burst.

Open the faucet so melting water has somewhere to go, then warm the pipe slowly with a hair dryer or a space heater placed safely away from anything that burns. Never use an open flame or a torch on a frozen pipe. If you see water, a bulge or frost on the pipe in a wall, shut off the main and call.

Before the next freeze, disconnect hoses and insulate exposed pipes. On a cold night, let a cabinet door stand open so room air reaches the pipes under a sink.

Spot repair makes sense when

  • Nearby pipe looks clean and passes a pressure test
  • The home is in its first years and the plumbing is original
  • You want the shortest repair with the smallest wall opening

Replacing a longer run makes sense when

  • Several pinholes or leaks appear along the same line
  • The pipe has been repaired before and failed again
  • The break exposes damaged or poorly supported tubing
  • You are already opening walls for a remodel

Cast iron drain pipe: honest limits in a city this new

Cast iron drain pipe is rare in a city where most homes date from the late 1990s on, since plastic drain pipe took over long before then. If you do have cast iron, it may be in an older commercial building or in a repair someone made with salvaged parts.

When cast iron cracks or rots through, the plumber replaces the damaged length with a modern pipe and the proper coupling. We would rather tell you the pipe is plastic and fine than sell you a replacement you do not need.

Questions about burst pipe repair in Eagle Mountain

Do you handle burst pipe repair emergencies at night or on weekends in Eagle Mountain?

Yes. Dispatch runs 24/7. Shut the main valve first, open the lowest faucet, then call (385) 488-1892 and we will plan the visit. Same-day appointments are offered when available for contained leaks.

What should I do first when a pipe bursts?

Close the main shutoff valve by turning it clockwise. Then drain the line at the lowest faucet, cut power to the water heater and move valuables away from the water.

Can a frozen pipe be thawed without a plumber?

Sometimes. Open the faucet, then warm the pipe slowly with a hair dryer. Never use a flame. If you see frost or a bulge inside a wall, or if the pipe has already split, shut off the main and call.

Is copper pipe repair different from PEX repair?

Yes. Copper is cut back and rejoined with soldered or press fittings, while PEX uses crimp or expansion fittings. Mixing methods without the right adapters invites a new leak.

How do I know a drain pipe is cracked and not just clogged?

A clog backs up. A crack leaks when water runs and leaves a stain or smell under the fixture. A camera or a pressure test settles it. For a clog, see drain cleaning.

Do I need a permit for a pipe repair?

Permit rules vary, so ask the Eagle Mountain Building Department at 801-789-6614. Ordinary repairs may be exempt, while major plumbing repairs and relocated lines can need one.

How do I check that a plumber is licensed?

The Utah Division of Professional Licensing lists apprentice, journeyman, residential journeyman, master and residential master licenses. Use the Verify a License lookup before work starts.
