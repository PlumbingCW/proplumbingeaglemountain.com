What this site collects

Eagle Mountain Pro Plumbing is a call-only website. There are no forms, accounts or checkout pages, so the site does not ask you to type personal details into it.

Like most websites, the hosting service may record technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. This information is used to keep the site running and to understand how pages perform.

Phone calls

When you call (385) 488-1892, the call is answered by dispatch. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

The phone number you call from, the details you share about your plumbing problem and your service address are used to arrange the visit you asked for. We ask only for what is needed to book and complete the work.

Cookies and third-party services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and an embedded map from Google Maps. Those services may set their own cookies or receive your IP address according to their own policies.

We do not run advertising pixels or social media trackers on this site.

Your choices

You can block cookies in your browser settings, and you can ask what we hold about a call by phoning the number above or writing to contact@proplumbingeaglemountain.com.
