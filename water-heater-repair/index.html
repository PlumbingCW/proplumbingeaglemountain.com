Where the water comes from

Water heater leaking from the base, the top, or the pipes

A puddle does not always mean the tank has failed. Fittings on top, the relief valve, and the drain valve can all drip while the steel tank is still sound, and those are repair jobs.

To find the source we dry every joint, wrap a dry paper towel around each one, and watch which spot darkens first. If the shell itself is weeping, the better path is a new tank.

Puddle under the base

Often the drain valve or the tank shell. A worn drain valve is a quick swap, but wet insulation under the jacket points to the tank.

Drip from the discharge pipe

The temperature and pressure relief valve is opening on heat or pressure, or its seat is worn. Pressure that climbs when the heater runs may need an expansion tank.

Wet fittings on top

Loose or corroded nipples, flex connectors, or a shutoff valve. These repairs are the most hopeful ones and save the tank.

Damp tank, no drip

Condensation can bead on a cold tank when it first heats. If the dampness returns once the tank is warm, treat it as a leak.

Hot water that runs out fast or never shows up

When a shower turns cold within minutes, the usual suspects are a dead lower element, a cracked dip tube sending cold water straight to the outlet, or a thermostat that no longer calls for heat. On a gas unit, check that the pilot is lit before assuming the worst. If the tank is silent and cold with the breaker on, call, because the next step needs a meter.

A tank that is failing for good may be a candidate for a tankless swap.

By heater type

Gas, electric, and hybrid heaters fail in different places

Each tank type has its own short list of wear parts, so the first diagnostic question is which kind you own. The data plate on the side lists fuel, capacity, and the model number we use to order parts.

Gas: pilot and thermocouple

A weak pilot flame or a worn thermocouple shuts the gas off. Newer units use an electronic igniter and flame sensor instead.

Gas: control valve and flue

A gas control valve that does not open, or a flue that spills exhaust, needs a technician. We test draft before we leave.

Electric: heating elements

Two elements, upper and lower, do the work. A failed lower element is the classic cause of hot water that fades quickly.

Electric: thermostats and limits

Each element has its own thermostat, and a high-limit switch cuts power on overheating. A stuck thermostat can also scald.

Hybrid: fan, filter, and condensate

A heat pump water heater needs clear airflow. Clogged filters and plugged condensate drains cause most shutdowns.

Hybrid: backup elements

These units fall back on electric elements when the heat pump stops. Fast-draining hot water can mean the heat pump has quit.

Worth knowing

Why a spent anode rod and sediment decide repair versus replace

Inside every steel tank, a magnesium or aluminum anode rod corrodes on purpose so the steel lining does not. Once the rod is eaten down to its core wire, the tank starts taking the attack itself. Pulling the rod shows how much protection remains.

Minerals in any water supply settle as sediment, insulating the burner or element from the water. That scale costs efficiency and causes the popping sound. If both the rod and the tank floor look bad, a new part only buys a short extension.

Questions about water heater repair in Eagle Mountain

Do you handle water heater repair emergencies at night or on weekends in Eagle Mountain?

Yes. Dispatch at (385) 488-1892 takes calls around the clock, and same-day appointments are available when the schedule allows. Shut off power or gas and close the cold supply while you wait. A gas smell means leave the house first.

Is a water heater leaking from the bottom always a dead tank?

No, though it often is. A bad drain valve or a loose fitting can drip onto the base and look like a tank leak. If the water comes from the seam or through the jacket, the tank has rusted through and replacement is the honest answer.

Why does my water heater pop or rumble when it heats?

Sediment on the tank floor traps water that boils under the scale, and the bursts make the noise. A flush can help a younger tank. A heavily scaled old tank usually keeps getting louder.

Why does my electric water heater keep tripping the breaker?

A shorted heating element or damaged wiring is the usual cause. Do not keep resetting it. A technician can test each element and find the one that leaks current.

Can a dripping relief valve discharge pipe be repaired without a new heater?

Sometimes. A worn relief valve can be replaced. If the valve is opening because pressure spikes as the tank heats, the plumbing also needs an expansion tank or a pressure check, or the new valve will drip too.

Should I fix my heater or switch to a tankless unit?

Fix it when the tank is sound and the part is minor. Think about a tankless unit when the tank is failing and you want continuous hot water, knowing the gas line and venting may need work.

Does water heater work need a permit in Eagle Mountain?

The city's permit sheet lists water heaters, and some minor repairs can be exempt. Permit rules vary, so ask the Eagle Mountain Building Department. We tell you what we find on your job.
