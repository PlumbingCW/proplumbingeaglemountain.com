Sewer line cleaning for a main line that will not drain

When every drain in the house runs slowly, the clog is probably in the main line, and that cannot wait long. Our 24/7 dispatch takes emergency sewer line cleaning calls in Eagle Mountain, UT. Stop using water and leave toilets unflushed while you wait for the plumber.

Sewer line cleaning means clearing a blockage out of the main drain, the large pipe that carries all household wastewater to the street connection. We use a heavy cable or a hydro jet through the cleanout. You may also hear it called sewer drain cleaning or main sewer cleaning, and the work is the same.

Sewage backing up? Do this before the plumber arrives

A full main line can push wastewater into the house in minutes. Dispatch handles nights and weekends, and a same-day visit may be open to you.

Take these steps right away.

  1. Stop the washer, dishwasher, showers and sinks at once.
  2. Leave toilets alone, and keep the lid down on any that bubble.
  3. Find the main cleanout cap, often outside near the house or in the garage or basement, and do not open it while the line is full.
  4. Keep children and pets out of any wet room, and wear gloves and boots if you must move things.
  5. Skip drain chemicals. Standing sewage dilutes them, and they splash on the plumber.
  6. Note the first fixture that backed up and the time it started.
The process

What the plumber does at the cleanout

1

Open the cleanout

We loosen the cap slowly, because a full line can release pressure and wastewater. Floor protection goes down first.

2

Cable to the clog

A heavy cable with a cutting head runs toward the street until it meets resistance. The length fed out tells us roughly how far the clog sits.

3

Cut and flush

The cutting head breaks up roots or packed waste, then we flush with water or run a hydro jet to carry the debris away.

4

Test and look

We run water through every fixture, then offer a camera run to check for a sag or a break.

Usual suspects

What clogs a main sewer line

Four causes account for most main line stoppages.

Roots

Fine roots enter joints and catch paper. In neighborhoods with young trees, the roots may only be starting.

Wipes and paper towels

They do not dissolve, and they knit into a mat at bends in the pipe.

Grease and sludge

Kitchen fat that passed through the house lines gathers in the lower main run.

A sag or offset joint

Solids drop out where water slows, so the clog keeps coming back.

Worth knowing

Why a cleaned line can clog again

Two causes explain most repeat calls. A sag collects solids at one spot, and roots grow back through an open joint. A camera shows which one applies, and a repair then deals with it, which our sewer line repair page covers.

Between cleanings, keep wipes out of the toilet and watch the lowest drain after heavy water use. If it slows twice in one season, ask for a camera run.

Questions about sewer line cleaning in Eagle Mountain

Do you handle sewer line cleaning emergencies at night or on weekends in Eagle Mountain?

Yes. Dispatch is staffed 24/7, holidays included, and a same-day visit can be booked if one is open. Stop water use, avoid flushing, and call (385) 488-1892.

What is the difference between sewer line cleaning and drain cleaning?

Drain cleaning clears a fixture or branch pipe, usually 1.5 to 2 inches wide. Sewer line cleaning clears the main line, usually 3 to 4 inches wide, from the house to the street. The tools are bigger, and the access is a cleanout instead of a trap. For smaller clogs, see drain cleaning.

Will cleaning the sewer line fix a backup for good?

It fixes a plain blockage. If a sag, roots or a cracked joint caused the clog, it can return in weeks. A camera run shows the cause and tells you whether you need only cleaning or a repair.

Why does the toilet bubble when the washing machine drains?

The washer pushes a lot of water down the line. If the main is partly blocked, air is forced back through the nearest open trap, which is often the toilet. Treat the bubbling as an early warning and book cleaning before it becomes a full backup.

Is the clog in my pipe or in the city main?

If only your house is affected, the clog is probably in your line. If neighbors also back up, the public main may be the cause. In that case call the city, since the main is the utility's responsibility.

Can I clean the main line myself with a rented machine?

Some people do, but a heavy machine can snag, break a pipe or kick back. A cleanout can also hold pressure and wastewater when the line is full. A plumber can pair the work with a camera so you see whether the pipe is sound.

Do I need a permit to clean a sewer line?

Cleaning is routine maintenance. If a repair follows, permit rules vary, so ask the Eagle Mountain Building Department at 801-789-6614.
