New construction plumbing, handled in order

A new house arrives with builder-selected fixtures and a basic water heater. Many owners later add a water softener, upgrade a faucet, install a second sink or swap the tank for a tankless unit. Each of those is a plumbing job that may need a permit.

Our plumbing installation page covers fixture and line work. Ask the Eagle Mountain Building Department at 801-789-6614 whether your job needs a permit, because the city permit sheet lists water heaters and new or relocated fixtures.

Side by side

Tank or tankless for a new Overland home

Neither is better for every house. The choice depends on how much hot water you use at once and what the home is set up for.

Tank water heater

  • Lower purchase cost at the start
  • Stores hot water, so a long shower can run it out
  • Needs floor space and a safe drain path
  • See water heater installation

Tankless water heater

  • Heats water as it flows
  • Needs correct gas and venting for the unit
  • Needs periodic descaling to keep working
  • See tankless water heaters

Questions from Overland homeowners

Do you serve the Overland community?

Dispatch covers Eagle Mountain, and Overland addresses are included. Call (385) 488-1892, give your street and phase name, and we confirm before sending anyone.

Can I install a water softener in a new house?

Usually yes, but the layout matters. Read the water softener page, test your tap, and check warranty terms and permit needs first.

Should I replace the builder water heater?

Only when it fails or no longer meets your hot water needs. A newer tank rarely needs replacing, and a plumber can check it first.

Who approves plumbing permits here?

Contact the Eagle Mountain Building Department, 3267 E Campus Dr, Suite H, or call 801-789-6614. Describe your project and they tell you what applies.

How do I confirm a plumber's license?

The state Verify a License lookup shows license class and status once you enter the plumber's name. Ask for the number before work starts.
