What plumbing wears first in a decade-old house

By the ten-year mark, a tank water heater has collected sediment, rubber seals in toilets have hardened and the first supply hoses may be stiff. None of that means the plumbing is failing, but a yearly look catches problems while they are small.

A drain that slows down now and then is worth attention before it blocks completely. Our drain cleaning page explains how cable and jet clearing differ.
