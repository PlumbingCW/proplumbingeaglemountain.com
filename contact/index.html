At a glance

Emergency call or routine call: which is yours

If you are unsure, call anyway and ask. The dispatcher will sort it out in a minute.

What to say on the call

Start with your street address and the nearest cross street. Then say what you see, hear or smell, such as a ceiling stain, a gurgling drain or a tank that is hissing. Mention whether you closed the main shutoff and whether anyone is in danger.

If you smell gas, leave the building first and call 911 or the gas utility. Phone us afterward. A gas smell is never something to troubleshoot from inside the house.

  • ✓Water heater type and age, if that is the issue

Why there are no forms

A form cannot tell you to close a valve while water is moving. A call can. That is why every request here goes through a phone line and not a web form or chat window.

If you are comparing providers, look at the services page to see what we handle. People in the next town east can read the Saratoga Springs page. The service area page lists the Eagle Mountain neighborhoods we dispatch to.

Who else to call for city-side problems

Some problems belong to the city, not a plumber. For water supply questions, the Eagle Mountain City Water Department is at 1650 E. Stagecoach Run. For permits on water heaters or major repairs, call the Building Department at 801-789-6614, since permit rules vary.

Before any digging in your yard, call 811 for Blue Stakes of Utah. If you plan to hire anyone for plumbing work, look up the license in the state's Verify a License lookup.

Common questions

Do you answer the phone at night?

Yes. Emergency calls are taken day and night at (385) 488-1892.

Can I send photos?

The dispatcher can tell you whether photos help. Give the situation by phone first, since a call can guide you through shutting the water off.

Is there a form on the site?

No. The site has none, and the phone is the only call to action.

What if the main valve will not close?

Tell the dispatcher at once. Keep power off near the water and move valuables, and the plumber will plan for a stuck valve.
