Before we arrive: if you have a burst pipe or active leak

The minutes before the plumber arrives decide how much water reaches your floors. These four steps work in any Saratoga Springs home.

Shut off the main valve

Turn the main shutoff clockwise until it stops. If it will not close, tell the dispatcher and call the city office at 801-766-9793 about the street-side valve.

Kill power to wet areas

Flip the breakers for rooms with standing water, but only if the panel is dry and you can reach it without stepping in water.

Clear the area

Move rugs, electronics and stored boxes away from the leak. Lift furniture legs off wet carpet and set foil or wood blocks under them.

Call the 24/7 line

Phone (385) 488-1892, give your address and cross street, and describe the source. Photos of the damage help your insurance claim later.

City water, pressurized irrigation and sewer: who handles what

The City of Saratoga Springs provides culinary water, pressurized irrigation and sanitary sewer. Because the systems are separate, a wet yard does not always mean a leak inside your house. Shut your indoor main and check the meter, and if it stops, the soggy strip may be an irrigation line.

Sewage backing up inside the house is a different case. If every drain is slow at once, the blockage may be in your lateral or in the city main. A camera inspection on your side shows which. The city office is at 319 S Saratoga Rd, 801-766-9793, if the main turns out to be the issue.

Garbage service in the city is run by Republic Services, so a jammed disposal is a plumbing problem, not a pickup problem. Hand-cranking the disposal's underside with the correct hex key frees most jams. If the unit hums but will not turn, our garbage disposal page explains the repair choices.
