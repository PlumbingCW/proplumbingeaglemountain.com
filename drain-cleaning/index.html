Drain cleaning for sinks, tubs and main lines

A drain that stops draining on a Friday night cannot wait for Monday. Eagle Mountain Pro Plumbing dispatches licensed and insured plumbers 24/7 for emergency drain cleaning in Eagle Mountain, UT. While you wait, stop running water, leave the toilets unflushed, and note which fixtures are slow.

Drain cleaning is the removal of a blockage from a drain pipe with a cable machine, a drain auger or high-pressure water, so wastewater flows downhill again. We ask where it backs up, pick the right tool, and tell you the clear price before the work begins.

Urgent

When a clogged drain is an emergency

Most clogs can wait for a morning appointment. A clog becomes an emergency when water has nowhere to go and will spill onto a floor, or when the main line backs up into the lowest drain in the house.

Same-day and after-hours calls are normal for this kind of work. Stop the water first, then follow the list below until the plumber arrives.

  1. Stop running sinks, showers, the dishwasher and the washing machine, because every gallon you add can rise in the lowest drain.
  2. Do not flush if the tub or a floor drain is gurgling. That sound points at the main line.
  3. Skip chemical drain openers. They sit above the clog, burn skin, and splash back at whoever opens the pipe.
  4. Move rugs, boxes and electronics away from the wet drain, and shut off power to anything standing in water.
  5. Use a plunger on a single toilet or sink, but stop the moment water rises in a second fixture.
  6. Write down which fixtures are slow or backing up. That tells the plumber where the clog sits.

Common causes

Why your drain slows down before it stops

A drain rarely blocks all at once. It narrows for weeks, and the cause usually matches the fixture.

Grease and food scraps

Cooking fat cools in the pipe and coats the wall, then catches coffee grounds and rice.

Hair and soap film

Tub and shower drains collect hair wrapped in soap, usually near the stopper or the trap.

Wipes and paper towels

Wipes sold as flushable do not fall apart like toilet paper, and they snag at bends.

Laundry lint

A washer standpipe is about 2 inches wide, and lint and fabric fibers can build up in it and overflow behind the machine.

Tree roots

Roots enter joints in the outdoor sewer line looking for moisture. Younger trees in newer neighborhoods may only be starting.

A blocked roof vent

A plugged vent makes drains gurgle and run slowly even though the pipe itself is open.

Kitchen sink clog: why it keeps coming back

A kitchen sink drain is usually 1.5 inches wide, with a P-trap under the cabinet that holds a little water against sewer gas. That curve is also where grease and food scraps settle first. If the dishwasher drains into the same line, dishwater can back up into the sink as well.

We start at the trap and the first stretch of wall pipe with a small drum cable, then work toward the branch line. If the sink clears but the clog returns in a few weeks, grease is likely coating a longer run of pipe. At that point hydro jetting cleans the wall in a way a cable cannot.

The process

How an emergency drain visit runs

1

Find the source

We ask which fixtures are slow and which are fine. A single sink points at a branch, while a tub and a toilet together point at the main.

2

Pick the access

We use the fixture trap, a cleanout or a vent, whichever gives the cleanest path to the clog.

3

Cut and clear

The cable head chews through the blockage, we pull the debris out, and we run water to test the flow.

4

Confirm and explain

If water runs freely, we explain the cause. If the flow stays weak, we suggest a camera look or a jet pass and give you the new price before more work starts.

Side by side

Snake it or jet it? A plain comparison

Cable (snake) cleaning

  • Opens a path through hair, paper and soft clogs
  • Fits small sink and tub lines
  • Suits a one-time blockage
  • Leaves a coating of grease on the pipe wall

Hydro jetting

  • Scours the full pipe wall with water
  • Suits grease, silt and repeat clogs
  • Needs a sound, well-joined pipe
  • Often follows a camera look first

A drain pipe that leaks is a different problem

A drain line leak does not look like a clog. Watch for a musty smell, a damp cabinet floor, a stain below a bathroom, or a drip that shows up only when the fixture drains. Water that appears after you empty the sink usually leaks from the drain side, not the supply side.

A cable cannot fix a cracked or loose joint, and pushing one through can make it worse. When the symptoms fit, we pair the visit with leak detection so the repair targets the right joint.

Worth knowing

A gurgling drain is often a vent problem

Every drain needs air behind the water. A vent pipe through the roof lets air in, so water slides out instead of pulling a vacuum. When a vent clogs with leaves, a nest or frost, nearby fixtures gurgle, drain slowly, and sometimes pull the trap dry, which lets sewer smell into the room.

If a cable clears the pipe but the gurgle stays, the vent is next. Utah winters bring hard freezes, and frost can narrow an exposed vent opening, so a gurgle that starts in a cold snap deserves a vent check.

Checklist

Keep the drains clear after the visit

A cleared drain stays clear longer with a few small habits.

  • ✓Scrape plates into the trash, and wipe greasy pans with a paper towel before washing.
  • ✓Pour cooled cooking fat into a can and put it in the bin.
  • ✓Fit a hair catcher in every shower and tub drain.
  • ✓Flush only toilet paper. Wipes marked flushable still belong in the bin.
  • ✓Run hot water for about half a minute after washing greasy dishes.
  • ✓Use a lint filter on the washer hose and empty it often.
  • ✓Find your main cleanout and keep the cap clear of snow and landscaping.
  • ✓Book a camera look if the same drain slows down twice in one season.

Questions about drain cleaning and unclogging in Eagle Mountain

Do you handle drain cleaning emergencies at night or on weekends in Eagle Mountain?

Yes. Dispatch answers around the clock, so a 2 a.m. backup or a Sunday overflow gets booked, and you can book a same-day slot if one is open. While you wait, stop running water and note which fixtures are slow.

Why does my kitchen sink keep clogging every few weeks?

Grease cools and sticks to the pipe wall, so each clog starts from a thin layer left behind. A cable opens a hole but does not scrub the wall. A jet pass cleans the full run, and a camera shows whether a low spot is holding grease. See hydro jetting for how that works.

Is it a sink clog or a main sewer clog?

One slow fixture usually means a local clog. If a tub, a toilet and a floor drain all act up at once, or water rises in the lowest drain when the washer runs, the main line is blocked. That case is covered on our sewer line cleaning page.

Can I use chemical drain cleaner before you arrive?

We advise against it. Caustic cleaners can sit in the pipe above a full clog and splash back on whoever opens it. A plunger and some patience are the safer first move.

Do you clear outdoor yard drains and grates?

Yes, when the drain connects to a pipe we can reach, such as a patio drain or a window well drain. Grates often hold leaves and silt, so we clear the opening first and then run a cable down the line. If the drain ties into a public system, ask the city about its rules.

Will you tell me the price before you start cutting?

Yes. The clear price comes before the first cut. If the cable finds something unexpected, such as a broken pipe, we stop, explain, and give you the new price before going further.

Does a clogged drain need a permit?

Ordinary clearing is routine maintenance and usually does not. Repairs to drain, waste and vent piping may. Permit rules vary, so ask the Eagle Mountain Building Department at 801-789-6614.
